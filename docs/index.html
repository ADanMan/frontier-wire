Today's five stories examine what is actually being tested behind a broad technical promise. The iPhone Duo teardown separates repair access from reliability, while the reef assessment distinguishes observed losses from future risk. Fresh ESA and NASA reports cover CRISTAL's ground tests, PACE's autumn-pigment measurements and Earth data for agriculture. Each result keeps its proper scale: a test is not a launch, a color map is not a diagnosis, and an agreement is not a finished tool.
