Она должна превращать тепло реактора в электричество, необходимое аппарату и демонстрации электрореактивного движения. В публикации назван замкнутый цикл Брайтона. ## English version

NASA has [announced contract actions for SR-1 Freedom's power module](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/glenn/sr-1-freedom-advances-with-progress-on-nuclear-power-module/). The October 20 release concerns a spacecraft intended to demonstrate nuclear-electric propulsion beyond Earth orbit. The immediate development assigns work on a key subsystem. It does not report a launch or a reactor already operating on this mission in space.

Sierra Lobo will provide the module's power-conversion system. Its role is to turn reactor heat into electricity for the spacecraft and its electric-propulsion demonstration. NASA identifies a closed Brayton cycle. At the functional level, these are separate stages: a source supplies heat, a conversion system produces electrical power, and propulsion uses that power. This description identifies which part of the overall system the current assignment advances.

The work falls under Space Flight Systems Development and Operations Contract III, awarded in 2024 to support spaceflight hardware development, testing and operations at Glenn Research Center. That underlying contract date should be distinguished from the current work assignment. The announcement is not evidence that the entire mission programme was first created this week.

Idaho National Laboratory, part of the US Department of Energy, is designing and developing the reactor. Integrating it with the conversion system will produce the complete nuclear power module. This division of responsibilities clarifies each participant's role. Selecting providers and coordinating their work does not establish that the integrated hardware has been manufactured, completed every test or become ready for space operations.

NASA describes interagency safety reviews, continuing oversight and a design process addressing hazards and failure tolerance. It also says the reactor will remain inactive until the spacecraft leaves Earth orbit. These describe intended development and operating arrangements; they are not an announcement that every future authorisation has been completed. The release provides no independent numerical risk assessment.

Launch remains scheduled for late 2028. Under the current plan, SR-1 Freedom will deliver SkyFall to Mars, carrying three helicopters that build on Ingenuity's approach. Scientific observations, exploration demonstrations and investigation of potential water sources belong to those vehicles' future work. Progress on a power-module contract cannot turn those objectives into results already obtained.

### Why it matters

A deep-space mission requires more than naming a powerful energy source. Conversion, propulsion, integration and validation must work together. This announcement makes one part of that chain more concrete. Subsequent progress can be assessed through hardware and testing milestones while keeping assigned work distinct from a completed demonstration.
