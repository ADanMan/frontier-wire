Today we examine how complex systems keep working: Deno announces its transition to Cloudflare, Ai2 reallocates GPU time, and NASA seeks commercial orbital-station plans. We also look at heat-shield experiments aboard a departing cargo spacecraft and the meaning of the Living Planet Index's 73% headline. Across five stories, we distinguish support from open code, occupancy from useful computation, testing from completed results, and an index from a count of animals.
