Today's five stories separate what is available, what has been measured and what remains promised. Kimi adds model access, MPS developers examine memory use, Swift leaves an archive after observations end, and a second colour makes plant luminescence more informative. In the revised Artemis plans, we examine each lander's route and the tests intended to precede a landing.
