Судя по описанию, ключевое отличие — более детальная работа со спутниковыми данными.

Стоит сразу зафиксировать: слово «беспрецедентный» — формулировка самой компании, а не независимая метрика. Но направление понятное: последние несколько лет ИИ-модели погоды (GraphCast от DeepMind, тот же WeatherNext) уверенно теснят традиционные численные методы по скорости расчёта, оставаясь конкурентоспособными по точности.

Для сельского хозяйства, авиации и коммунальных служб такая точность на уровне часов и километров — это не косметика, а реальные деньги.

Google [rolled out](https://www.theverge.com/tech/988921/weather-forecast-ai-model-google-satellite-update) an update to its AI weather forecasting model, saying the new WeatherNext 3 can produce forecasts at "unprecedented resolution," particularly for predicting rain and snowfall. The stated improvement centers on more detailed use of satellite data.

Worth flagging upfront: "unprecedented" is Google's own word, not an independently verified metric. The digest doesn't include specific accuracy numbers, comparisons against the prior model version, or against traditional physics-based forecasting systems like ECMWF, so it's too early to size up how big the real jump is. The trend, though, is clear — over the past few years, AI weather models (DeepMind's GraphCast, and WeatherNext itself) have been closing in on, and in some cases beating, traditional numerical methods on speed while staying competitive on accuracy.

The practical difference for an ordinary user: where a weather app used to say "rain possible this afternoon," a more granular model could in theory narrow that down to your specific neighborhood rather than the one next door. For agriculture, aviation, and utilities, that kind of hour-and-kilometer precision isn't cosmetic — it translates into real money.

### Why it matters

AI weather models are moving from research curiosity to infrastructure billions of people touch through ordinary apps. The open question is whether Google (and its competitors) will publish independently verifiable benchmarks, rather than marketing phrases like "unprecedented resolution."
