## Editorial

Today's four stories examine evidence left by craft, landscapes and software. A Sanam workshop connects objects with production, Landsat records autumn in Bieszczady, and two LLM updates distinguish dependency failures from debugging behaviour. In Beam's announcement, we separate licensing, computational requirements and the limits of small demonstrations.
