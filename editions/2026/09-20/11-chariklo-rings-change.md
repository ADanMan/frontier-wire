The very existence of rings around such a small object was a surprise when they were discovered: rings were thought to be a feature of giant planets like Saturn or Uranus, not tiny distant bodies.

The fact that the rings are actually changing, not just sitting there statically, matters more than the fact that they exist at all. For science, that makes Chariklo more than a curiosity discovered once and filed away — it's an active laboratory: a small body with a ring system that can be watched evolving over years, rather than reconstructed after the fact from a single snapshot of a giant planet.
