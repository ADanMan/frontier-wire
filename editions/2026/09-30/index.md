## Editorial

Today is about power renaming things while the world keeps doing its own thing. Morocco got its first woman prime minister, while Washington is teaching officials to stop saying "artificial intelligence" out loud. Meanwhile Saturn keeps spraying out ice chemistry nobody has fully explained, UK mortgage numbers quietly worsen, a theatre in London puts Keynes centre stage, and police finally catch up with an alleged hacker.
