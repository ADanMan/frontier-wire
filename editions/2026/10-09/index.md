## Editorial

This morning is about measurements that help test an attractive promise. Falcon ASR reports results on specified speech datasets, ML Intern describes training with baselines and budgets, and statisticians examine what human hours mean on an AI-capability chart. In space, SSPICY prepares for future inspections; in collectible cards, researchers check whether rare-card odds are disclosed. Across five stories, we keep evaluation boundaries, event dates and the distinction between plans and outcomes explicit.
