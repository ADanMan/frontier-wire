Пока это план тинктанка, а не официальная программа партии — но раз документ называют влиятельным, есть смысл считать его индикатором, куда может сдвинуться фискальная риторика Reform UK, если партия продолжит расти в опросах.

## English version

A thinktank linked to Nigel Farage's Reform UK is preparing a policy plan calling for the abolition of inheritance tax and the phasing out of capital gains tax, [The Guardian reports](https://www.theguardian.com/politics/2026/sep/16/reform-thinktank-tax-cuts-rich-centre-for-a-better-britain). The group, the Centre for a Better Britain, is drafting what's described as an influential plan — one built around substantial tax cuts specifically for wealthy Britons.

The pattern is familiar: a party that campaigns on working-class discontent and anti-establishment anger is simultaneously shaping a fiscal agenda that mainly benefits people at the top. Inheritance tax has long been a flashpoint in British politics — a minority of households actually pay it, yet it remains a symbolic battleground between those who see it as redistribution and those who call it a "death tax."

For now this is a thinktank's proposal, not official party policy — but being called influential is worth noting as a signal of where Reform UK's fiscal rhetoric could head if the party keeps climbing in the polls.

### Why it matters

If Reform UK is indeed drifting toward this kind of tax agenda, it reshapes British politics: a party gaining ground on anti-establishment anger is floating solutions that primarily benefit the wealthy.
